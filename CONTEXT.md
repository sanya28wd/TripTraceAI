# TripTrace Lost-Property Context

## Language

**Driver acknowledgement**:
The driver's acknowledgement of an item-check alert by opening it for review. It does not confirm that an item was found, secured, or belongs to a passenger.
_Avoid_: Acceptance of ownership, item confirmation

**Secured item**:
An item the driver has confirmed is physically secure. Its relationship to a passenger's report remains under review.
_Avoid_: Matched item, verified passenger property
